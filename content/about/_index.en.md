### Bridging the Gap Through Education & Mentorship

Talent is distributed equally, but opportunity is not. Prarthona exists to close
that gap for students whose families cannot carry the cost of an education.

In Bangladesh, underrepresented and underprivileged communities — particularly
Hindu religious minorities — face severe economic hardships, social
vulnerabilities, and systemic persecution. We believe that education is the
ultimate tool for empowerment, social resilience, and sustainable progress. Our
purpose is to make the educational path as smooth and accessible as possible for
these deserving scholars.

### How We Work

- **Full Support & Scholarships:** We fund school and university fees to
  eliminate financial barriers.
- **Personalized Mentorship:** We pair each student with a dedicated mentor who
  stays with them year after year, providing academic, personal, and career
  guidance.
- **End-to-End Commitment:** We track and support our scholars' progress
  continuously until they graduate.

Small in scale, deliberately personal, and focused on seeing every student
through to the end — we build a foundation for future leaders who will lift up
their communities.
